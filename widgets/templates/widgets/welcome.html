HIPPOCAMPUS

Six small tools for getting through your college applications.
Free, no account, nothing saved anywhere but your own browser.

Word Counter Counts the way the Common App counts — which is not the way Google Docs counts. Focus Write A blank page and nothing else. Full screen, no navigation, no reason to look away. Advice A short, opinionated reading list — the things actually worth your time before you apply. Resources Where to look things up, plus the soundtrack I wrote most of my own applications to.
